3 · How the score works, and why breadth plus depth

JevBench averages four axes with a harmonic mean, then multiplies by (Intelligence ÷ 50)² when Intelligence is under 50. Below 50, every point of Intelligence is worth about 1.5 points of score. That penalty is the whole story for small models.

score = 4 / (1/I + 1/C + 1/S + 1/K) × (I/50)² if I < 50  ·  a 2B is priced at the 4B hosted rate, so Cost ≈ 61 for everyone in the class

Malkuth's data is all short classification tasks, so it wins the easy tiers but scores 24% on the sealed set and 53 on Calibration. Kev's hard-family and real-document data is what moves the hard tier and Calibration. Nobody at 2B has trained on both; Step 5 does.
